# D3 digest: quality control and refinement (round 1, accessed 2026-10-27)
Caveat: most facts came through WebFetch/WebSearch summaries of pages (small-model extraction), not full-text reads. Treat numbers as "as extracted"; verify in the papers before relying.

## Findings
- **[LB]** Cascade: small model + QE deferral to large model. Tower-v2 7B -> 70B, deferral via CometKiwi (wmt22-da 0.5B, wmt23-xxl 10.5B) and MetricX-24 QE. Matching the 70B needs deferring 30-50% of examples (abstract + human eval) or 50-60% (MetricX auto eval, sec 4.2; paper is internally inconsistent). Cost parity: cascade costs more than always-large above 89% (CometKiwi-22), 75% (xxl), 86% (EuroLLM 9B), 97% (EuroLLM 1.7B) deferral. No explicit % savings reported. 11 pairs incl. en-zh, ja-zh; no Vietnamese. · https://arxiv.org/html/2502.12701v1 (EMNLP 2025, Farinhas et al.) · ACL/arXiv · 2025 · medium-high · quality-number/cost-number
- **[LB]** TEaR (translate, estimate, refine), single LLM, 3 calls per flagged segment: gains are small and model-dependent. GPT-3.5-turbo Zh-En COMET 78.67->79.50 (+0.83), BLEU -0.34; Gemini-Pro +0.60 COMET, BLEU -2.33; Claude-2 +8.22 COMET. One round is best: COMET drifts 79.50->79.20 by round 5. Cross-model refine sometimes hurts (Gemini-Pro refining Zh-En -0.48 COMET; Claude-2 En-De -1.14). Models are old (GPT-3.5, 2023); GPT-4-0613 gains tiny (BLEURT +0.12, 50 cases). Human preference small sample: GPT-3.5 IT won 4, tie 11, TEaR 9. No cost figures. · https://arxiv.org/html/2402.16379v3 · Findings NAACL 2025 · 2025 (arXiv 2024) · medium · quality-number/failure-mode
- Refinement is applied only to segments the estimator flags; flagged 94-160 of the 200-sample sets (Table 17), so roughly half the segments still get 3 calls. Ice-relevant: the gate matters for cost. · same URL · medium · method/cost-number
- **[LB]** GEMBA-MQM V2 (WMT25 metrics task, GPT-4.1-mini, reference-free): MQM error spans scored with 25/5/1 weights, minor punctuation 0.1; each segment scored 10 times, outliers beyond 2 sigma dropped, reciprocal-rank weighted mean; claims rank 1 by average correlation on WMT24 MQM test sets. 10x sampling per segment = ~10x judge cost; no cost figures given, no per-language numbers. · https://aclanthology.org/2025.wmt-1.67/ · WMT 2025 · 2025 · medium (claim self-reported) · quality-number/method

## Answers per question
1. Reference-free QE: in WMT24, XCOMET/MetricX (~0.72 avg corr) and prompted-LLM judges (~0.71) are close; GEMBA-MQM V2 (GPT-4.1-mini) claims first place but needs ~10 samples/segment. Vietnamese is not in WMT24 MQM sets and no Vietnamese-specific validation was found; judges are weaker in low-resource languages. COMET-Kiwi is cheapest per segment but not verified for Vietnamese here.
2. Second pass gains are small and uneven (TEaR: +0.6 to +0.8 COMET on GPT-3.5/Gemini, BLEU can fall; one round best; cross-model refine can hurt). Over-editing evidence is indirect: more rounds drift down, and constrained-edit designs are proposed to limit drift. No 2026-model numbers obtained.
3. Only direct measured cascade result: defer 30-50% (up to 60%) of segments by CometKiwi to match the large model; savings real below ~75-89% deferral. Not tested on Vietnamese or on frontier API models.
4. Heuristics exist (paragraph retention, length ratio, segment count) but published precision is poor for length ratio alone (32/1,258 genuine); no solid failure rates for omitted/merged segments in API LLM batches found.
5. One small literary PE study shows time reduction vs from-scratch; error taxonomy of what translators fix in literary LLM output not found.

## Findings
- **[LB]** Source-primed multi-turn doc translation gives small gains. With GPT-4o-mini the gain over single-turn is +0.53 dBLEU and over segment-level +1.34 COMET. - **[LB]** QE cascade (Farinhas et al.): the small model translates everything, QE ranks, and the worst outputs go to the large model. It matches Tower-v2 70B while deferring 30-50% (abstract and human eval, en-es and en-ja, 500 sources, 0-100 DA+SQM) or 50-60% (MetricX auto eval). Break-even deferral (cost parity with the large model alone) is 89% (cometkiwi-da 0.5B) and 75% (cometkiwi-da-xxl 10.5B); 97% and 86% with EuroLLM 1.7B and 9B as the small model. The whole WMT24 test set per pair is one batch for ranking. Models are Tower-v2 (open models); this was not tested with API models or Vietnamese. · https://arxiv.org/html/2502.12701 · arXiv/EMNLP 2025 · conf: medium-high · class: quality number · read: full text
- **[LB]** Refinement with current models (Tan et al., "What Does LLM Refinement Actually Improve?", doc-level literary, WMT24-Literary, 7 pairs: en-cs/de/es/ja/ru/zh, ja-zh; judge Claude 3.5 Sonnet v2 MQM-FSP). Doc-level MT beats segment-level (GPT-5.2 83.4 to 90.0; DeepSeek-V3 80.5 to 85.7). Segment-level refinement of doc-MT gains: GPT-5.2 +2.0, GPT-4o +1.9, DeepSeek-V3 +3.6, Qwen3-235B +2.6. The simple "general" refine prompt works best, while eval-then-refine, step-by-step (negative human MQM: GPT-5.2 -7.2, DeepSeek-V3 -4.2) and monolingual (accuracy -3.8) are worse or harmful. Human MQM for "general" refine: GPT-5.2 +5.6, DeepSeek-V3 +7.8. Gains come from fluency and style/terminology; accuracy gains are small or negative (human accuracy deltas -2.3 to +3.0). Pairwise: refined wins fluency 97.5% and style+terminology 93.1% of non-ties, accuracy 50.0% refined / 15.7% initial / 34.3% tie. Failure modes: segment-level refinement edits 25-40% of tokens (over-editing); self-diagnosis is unreliable (eval-refine flags 1.3-3.0x more errors than the judge, span F1 0.06-0.11); model confidence does not predict edits (AUC ~0.50); the source-conditioned score falls (drift from the source). ## Per-question answers
1. Source-primed multi-turn beats segment-level modestly (GPT-4o-mini +0.69 dBLEU, +1.34 COMET; small open models more) and single-turn by +0.5 to +2.5 dBLEU. Single-turn long docs show omissions (qualitative only, no rate). No measured cost; the saving rests on prefix caching in theory.
2. Reasoning lowers XCOMET-XL by 0.3 to 3.8 points across 4 models on 9 en-X pairs; Vietnamese is not covered, so applicability to vi is unverified.
3. Current models (GPT-5.2, GPT-4o, DeepSeek-V3): a single simple "refine" pass gives +2 to +4 MQM-FSP, with human MQM +5.6 for GPT-5.2; gains are fluency/style, not accuracy; the failure modes are over-editing and accuracy drift, and a structured "find errors, then fix" scheme is not better. QE cascade confirmed: defer 30-50% (human) / 50-60% (auto); the break-even is 75-89% for cometkiwi-based QE.
4. No measured per-segment omission/merge rate found for numbered/tagged batching. Only qualitative evidence: long single-turn docs omit, and quality degrades at 4k-8k-token chunks for most open models.
5. Not found as a category distribution. Indirect: refinement gains concentrate in fluency, style and terminology (so those are what remains to be fixed in a first draft), with accuracy errors persisting; MQM-Chat shows human annotators focus on omission/addition/grammar/inconsistency (chat domain, snippet).

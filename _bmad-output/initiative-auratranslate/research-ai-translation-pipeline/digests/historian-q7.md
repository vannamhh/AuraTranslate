In every commit touching it (915f1af, d31e95b, c52c88d, b61e87e, 521636e) "lô" means a list of segments each translated by its own request, same `PreparedTranslateCall` shape. Why one-per-segment was chosen is not stated in commit messages; the likely explanation sits in spec 4.9 (not read, excluded). Reasons visible in code/commits: per-segment `Token`/`Done {segment_id, usage}` events streamed to the grid, per-sentence cancel, "first error stops the batch and names the sentence" (batch_stopped_error carries segment_id), per-segment prompt assembly with Glossary/TM injection (6de227d Smart RAG pure function; b61e87e: batch reads TM rows once then scores each sentence while holding OpenWorkState).
